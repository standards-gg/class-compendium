GEAR & WEAPONS

When two weapons have similar DPS, take the slower one: Holy Strike and Seal of Command both scale with weapon damage.

Weapon speed decides your Seal: slower than 3.8 seconds per swing means Seal of Command, faster means Seal of Righteousness.

Human order: Strength, Spirit, Agility, Intellect, Stamina. Undead order: Strength, Agility, Intellect, Stamina, Spirit.

THINGS TO REMEMBER

Mana only regenerates after 5 seconds without spending any, so cast several spells back to back and let it trickle in.

Use your utility: Blessing of Freedom, Sacrifice and Protection, Purify and Hammer of Justice for yourself and your party.

Group buffs matter: Blessing of Kings, Might and Wisdom plus Devotion and Retribution Aura make the whole party stronger.
