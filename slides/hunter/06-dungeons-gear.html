DUNGEON & GROUP TIPS

A tank holds threat, so you can use Rapid Fire with less worry about pulling aggro.

Keep Hunter's Mark on the main target and let the tank get threat before Multi-Shot.

Explosive Trap and Frost Trap deal big area damage or slow a group so the tank can kite.
