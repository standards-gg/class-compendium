GEAR & WEAPONS

Take items with Agility first and the other stats when they show up.

Keep your bow or gun upgraded: ranged weapon damage is your main source of damage.

THINGS TO REMEMBER

Bring plenty of ammo when questing or entering dungeons.

Tame a boar early (its Charge builds threat and it is not a picky eater), then a Tallstrider for Dust Cloud armor penetration.

Kite enemies you pull off your pet: shoot, move a little, repeat.

Summon Hawk shares a cooldown with Arcane Shot. If it underwhelms, or a fight is nearly over, use Arcane Shot instead.
