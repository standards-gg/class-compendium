DUNGEON & GROUP TIPS

Frost Mages bring excellent single-target and area damage and can slow mobs to a crawl.

Your food, water and Arcane Intellect are valued by the whole party.

Polymorph buys time; Frost Nova works too but frozen enemies attack their nearest target.

Swap to Mage Armor instead of Ice or Frost Armor: you should not be tanking.
