THINGS TO REMEMBER

Keep Arcane Intellect and Frost Armor (or Ice Armor) up at all times; swap to Mage Armor in dungeons when you are not tanking.

Downrank Blizzard, Frost Nova and Cone of Cold to save mana: you use them for the slow, not the damage.

Skip spells that Frost does not use, like Scorch, to save gold. Visit the trainer for higher ranks of your damage spells.

Keep Light Feathers and buy reagents such as Rune of Teleportation. Stock mana potions and conjured mana gems for longer uptime.

Buff passing players with Arcane Intellect: it costs you little and may be returned.
