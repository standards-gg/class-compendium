GEAR & WEAPONS

Spell Power is already on low-level gear in Forever, so you can stack a decent amount early.

Haste is not on gear yet, and your DoTs and Drains are not affected by it.

At this level, the Blackfathom Deeps quest wand (Gravestone Scepter) is described as overpowered: rely on it over Shadow Bolt.

Consumables from the Auction House: Lesser Arcane Elixir (15 Spell Power), Lesser Mageblood Elixir (mp5), Elixir of Wisdom, Briny Seafood Stew (14 Spell Power, 5% XP).

THINGS TO REMEMBER

Visit your trainer every two levels (2, 4, 6 ...) and re-check your action bars after training so you use the new rank.

Keep your armor buff up: Demon Skin early, Demon Armor at level 20 (they do not stack).

Let your DoTs finish the job: do not spend mana on another spell when they will already kill the target.

Always carry Soul Shards: Drain Soul from level 10; shards fuel demons, Healthstones, Soulstones and Ritual of Summoning.

Class quests unlock your demons: Voidwalker at 10, Succubus at 20 (plus Staff of Soran'ruk, Orb and a Soul Pouch), Felhunter at 30.

Use Life Tap only when you have enough health to do it safely, and carry food and water anyway.
