DUNGEON & GROUP TIPS

Curse of the Elements raises your group's magic damage; Curse of Recklessness raises physical damage. Pick by group on bosses and elites.

Use Summon Succubus for damage once you unlock her at level 20, or Voidwalker if you prefer safety.

You can summon allies with Ritual of Summoning when two party members help click the portal.

Soulstone and Healthstone support the whole party.
