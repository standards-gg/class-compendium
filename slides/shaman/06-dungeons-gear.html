DUNGEON & GROUP TIPS

Fire Nova on 3+ targets is one of the best spells in the game, but mana-hungry: if you cannot drink, spend mana on Fire Nova first.

Use Earth Shock only for casts that must be kicked, such as Fear or Healing Touch.

Tremor Totem ticks every 4 seconds: drop it as the Fear lands, not before.
