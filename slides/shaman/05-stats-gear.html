2 attack power per point: your best stat.

Weak: Shaman spells scale at about 1/5 of other classes.

GEAR & WEAPONS

Weapon: any two-hander with as much damage as possible. Once you have Stormstrike, take the slowest weapon you can find.

If you tank, use a shield and a good one-hander; otherwise a shield is not worth it.

Level 20 consumables: Longjaw Mud Snapper (10 AP, 5% XP), Elixir of Ogre Strength, Elixir of Lesser Agility, Elixir of Wisdom, Heavy Sharpening Stone and Heavy Weightstone.

THINGS TO REMEMBER

Use the mp5 rule: mana regenerates 5 seconds after your last spell, so front-load your casts and then just melee. Spend freely if you want more damage at the cost of mana efficiency.

Keep Flametongue Weapon up (Rockbiter makes a tank's life harder; some mobs resist fire). At level 30 use Windfury Weapon.

Strength of Earth Totem is weak at low level but cheap with 30 yards of range: pre-place it before packs or a boss.

Totemic Projection moves your totems, but has a 1 minute cooldown: use it sparingly.

Mana potions are underrated for uptime.
