GEAR & WEAPONS

Watch for wand upgrades, especially at low levels: a good wand is your best friend.

Wand finishing combines with Spirit Tap and the MP5 rule to cut downtime.

THINGS TO REMEMBER

Visit your class trainer every 2 levels (2, 4, 6, 8, 10 ...) so you do not miss upgrades.

Follow the MP5 rule: mana only regenerates after 5 seconds without casting. Pulling several enemies is possible but hurts your sustain.

Priest spells are mana-intensive: do not overkill with a damage spell when a mob is nearly dead, and consider Psychic Scream instead of re-shielding.

Sustain is a big part of leveling: less drinking means less downtime.
