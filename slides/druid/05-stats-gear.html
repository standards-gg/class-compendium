GEAR & WEAPONS

A high-DPS weapon is now extremely important because shapeshifted attacks scale with weapon damage.

Druids can now wield polearms.

THINGS TO REMEMBER

Cat and Bear Form now scale with weapon damage and use a new Feral Combat weapon skill: a high-DPS weapon matters.

Mana and energy regenerate continuously, so it is easier to spend down to 0 energy and less important to time casts.

Faerie Fire and Nature's Grasp are baseline; combo points transfer between targets; Druids can use polearms.

5 second rule: mana regeneration only starts after 5 seconds without spending mana, so batch your spells then shapeshift while it regenerates.

New abilities arrive on every even level: go back to the trainer every other level.
