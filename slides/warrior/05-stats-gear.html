GEAR & WEAPONS

Upgrade your weapon whenever you can: it is the biggest lever for a Warrior.

Prefer faster weapons in Protection: Revenge and Shield Slam do not scale with weapon damage.

Take Blacksmithing and always use Sharpening Stones (free damage; faster weapons benefit more).

Sword and shield are the default setup for Protection.

THINGS TO REMEMBER

Rage is the resource: you are far stronger at 50 Rage than at 0, so hop from fight to fight without dropping to zero.

Warriors are weak against several enemies until Protection with decent gear: hit one or two and Hamstring-kite the rest.

Take First Aid and keep levelling it; Victory Rush only triggers after a kill, so bandages cover the gaps.

Train weapon skill on low-level mobs when you pick up a new weapon type: weapon skill is your chance to hit.
