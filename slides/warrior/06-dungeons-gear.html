DUNGEON & GROUP TIPS

Highest threat: Revenge, Sunder Armor, Heroic Strike / Cleave, Rend, Thunder Clap (higher on groups).

Pull casters around a corner so they run into melee, or open with your ranged weapon on tricky pulls.

Charge in and keep walking when you are speed-running a dungeon.

Sunder bosses to 5 stacks. Demoralizing Shout currently gives no threat, so do not open with it.
