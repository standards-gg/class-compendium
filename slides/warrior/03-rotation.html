CORE PRIORITIES

Protection from level 16: Revenge and Shield Slam hit hard, and Shield Specialization plus Master of Defense keep the Rage flowing.
RevengeCast it whenever it procs; it is the main damage skill from 16.
Shield BlockUse on cooldown, just before enemies hit you, to enable Revenge.
Sunder ArmorKeep stacking it until the target is around 50% HP (5 stacks on elites).
Battle ShoutKeep it up at all times.
Victory RushUse before the kill buff expires, but skip it above 90% HP.
